# Influencer Marketing
<!-- nav_order: 80 -->

Influencer marketing involves partnering with individuals who have established audiences on social platforms to promote your products or services. The industry has matured significantly — pricing now reflects format complexity and engagement quality over follower counts, and compliance enforcement has intensified.

## Types of Influencer Collaboration

### 1. Sponsored Posts

- **Feed Posts:** Influencers share static or video content featuring your product.
- **Stories / Ephemeral Content:** 24-hour placements; lower cost, but no permanent record.

### 2. Short-Form Video

- **TikTok Videos / Instagram Reels:** High-reach vertical video. Most effective format for product discovery with younger demographics.
- **YouTube Shorts:** Growing inventory; lower rates than long-form integrations.

### 3. Long-Form Integrations

- **YouTube Dedicated Videos or Mid-Roll Mentions:** Higher trust and retention; significantly higher rates.

### 4. Product Reviews and Unboxing

- **Organic-feeling demos:** Showcase features, first impressions, or comparisons.

### 5. Giveaways and Contests

- **Engagement campaigns:** Influencers host contests to distribute your products, boosting followers and visibility.

### 6. Product Seeding

- Send free product to nano/micro influencers without a paid contract. Many smaller creators will post about a gifted product when it genuinely fits their audience, which makes seeding one of the cheapest ways for a small business to start.

---

## How Creators Price Their Work

Creator rates depend on the format (a video costs more than a story), engagement rate, audience location, niche, and any exclusivity or usage rights you ask for, not just follower count. Creators are usually grouped as nano (1,000 to 10,000 followers) or micro (10,000 to 100,000).

There is no reliable public rate card, so we do not list prices. Ask each creator for a media kit with recent reach and engagement, request quotes from several creators in the same tier, and agree in writing on deliverables, usage rights, and disclosure before you pay.

## Platforms for Finding and Managing Influencers

### Budget-Friendly Options

- **[Elev8or](https://www.elev8or.io/):** Free starter tier and paid monthly plans. Includes creator discovery, fake follower audits, and automated campaign workflows.
- **[Collabstr](https://collabstr.com/):** Marketplace model with no monthly subscription; it charges a fee on each transaction. Optimal for one-off or occasional campaigns.
- **[Skeepers](https://skeepers.io/):** Designed for high-volume product seeding; enables small teams to coordinate hundreds of nano-creator gifted campaigns.

## FTC Disclosure Requirements

Civil penalties for violating FTC rules can reach [$53,088 per violation](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2025/01/17/2025-01361/adjustments-to-civil-penalty-amounts) (the 2025 inflation-adjusted maximum, which the FTC kept for 2026).

### What the FTC asks for

From the FTC's [Disclosures 101 for Social Media Influencers](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/disclosures-101-social-media-influencers):

- **Hard to miss:** The disclosure goes with the endorsement itself, not only in a profile, at the end, or behind a "more" link.
- **Plain words:** "Ad", "advertisement", "sponsored", `#ad`, or `#sponsored`. Avoid `#sp`, `#spon`, `#collab`, and stand-alone "thanks" or "ambassador".
- **Video:** Put the disclosure in the video, not just the description; saying it and showing it on screen is most noticeable. Repeat it during livestreams.
- **Platform labels:** Use tools like Instagram's "Paid partnership" label in addition to, not instead of, the creator's own disclosure.

### Written disclosure policy
Give every creator a short written disclosure policy before the campaign starts, and check their posts. See [Legal and Ethical Considerations](../resources/LEGAL.md) for the wider rules.

## Benefits

- **Trust and credibility:** Leverages influencers' existing audience relationships.
- **Extended reach:** Access new, engaged demographics organically.
- **Authentic content:** Creator-generated content outperforms brand-produced ads on native platforms.
- **Cost efficiency:** Nano/micro tiers and product seeding offer strong ROI relative to traditional paid media.

---

## Setting Up Influencer Marketing

1. **Define objective:** Product awareness, conversion, user-generated content (UGC), or community building.
2. **Identify creators:** Use a platform like Elev8or or Collabstr; filter by engagement rate (not follower count), audience location, and niche.
3. **Vet for authenticity:** Run fake follower checks; review past sponsored post performance.
4. **Set terms in writing:** Deliverables, timeline, usage rights (especially for repurposing as paid ads), and disclosure requirements.
5. **Brief but don't script:** Provide talking points, key claims, and brand guidelines — over-scripting kills authenticity.
6. **Track performance:** Use unique UTM links or discount codes; request creator analytics screenshots post-campaign.
