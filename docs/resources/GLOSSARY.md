# Glossary
<!-- nav_order: 60 -->

Plain-language definitions of the abbreviations used across these guides. Each entry links to the guide where the term matters most.

## Costs and results

| Term | Stands for | What it means | Where it comes up |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| CPA | Cost per acquisition | What you pay, on average, for one sale or sign-up. Total ad spend divided by the number of conversions. | [Analytics](ANALYTICS.md) |
| CPC | Cost per click | What you pay each time someone clicks your ad. The usual pricing model for search and many social ads. | [SEA](../channels/SEA.md), [Budgeting](BUDGETING.md) |
| CPE | Cost per engagement | What you pay for each like, comment, share, or other interaction with a social post. | [Social](../channels/SOCIAL.md) |
| CPL | Cost per lead | What you pay for each contact, form fill, or sign-up you can follow up with. | [Budgeting](BUDGETING.md) |
| CPM | Cost per mille | What you pay for 1,000 ad impressions ("mille" is Latin for thousand). Used for display, video, and out-of-home. | [Display](../channels/DISPLAY.md), [Budgeting](BUDGETING.md) |
| CPV | Cost per view | What you pay each time someone watches your video ad past the platform's view threshold. | [Video](../channels/VIDEO.md) |
| CTR | Click-through rate | The share of people who saw your ad and clicked it. Clicks divided by impressions. | [Analytics](ANALYTICS.md) |
| LTV | Lifetime value | The total profit you expect from one customer over the whole relationship. It tells you how much you can afford to pay to win a customer. | [Budgeting](BUDGETING.md) |
| PPC | Pay per click | Any ad you pay for only when it is clicked. Often used to mean search ads. | [SEA](../channels/SEA.md) |
| ROAS | Return on ad spend | Revenue earned for each dollar spent on ads. $4 of sales from $1 of ads is a ROAS of 4. | [Analytics](ANALYTICS.md) |
| ROI | Return on investment | Profit compared with what you spent. Unlike ROAS, it subtracts your costs, not just ad spend. | [Budgeting](BUDGETING.md) |

## Channels and formats

| Term | Stands for | What it means | Where it comes up |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| CTV | Connected TV | Streaming TV watched on an internet-connected television, such as through a smart TV or streaming stick. Ads can be bought with small budgets and targeted like digital ads. | [Broadcast](../channels/BROADCAST.md), [Video](../channels/VIDEO.md) |
| DOOH | Digital out-of-home | Screens in public places, such as digital billboards, transit displays, and screens in stores. Often bought programmatically by the hour or by audience. | [OOH](../channels/OOH.md) |
| OOH | Out-of-home | Any advertising people see outside their homes: billboards, transit, street furniture, and venue screens. | [OOH](../channels/OOH.md) |
| PMax | Performance Max | A Google Ads campaign type that uses automation to place one campaign across Search, YouTube, Display, Gmail, and Maps. | [SEA](../channels/SEA.md) |
| SEA | Search engine advertising | Paid ads shown on search results pages. | [SEA](../channels/SEA.md) |
| SERP | Search engine results page | The page a search engine shows after someone searches. Search ads appear on it. | [SEA](../channels/SEA.md) |
| UGC | User-generated content | Photos, videos, and reviews made by customers or creators rather than the brand. Often reused in ads. | [Influencer](../channels/INFLUENCER.md) |

## Buying and tracking

| Term | Stands for | What it means | Where it comes up |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| ATT | App Tracking Transparency | Apple's iOS prompt that asks users whether an app may track them across other apps and websites. Many users say no, which limits ad tracking. | [Legal](LEGAL.md), [FAQ](FAQ.md) |
| CAPI | Conversions API | A way to send conversion data from your server straight to an ad platform, such as Meta, instead of relying only on a browser pixel. | [Social](../channels/SOCIAL.md) |
| CRM | Customer relationship management | Software that stores your customer and lead records, such as contact details and purchase history. | [Analytics](ANALYTICS.md) |
| DSP | Demand-side platform | Software that lets advertisers buy ad space across many websites, apps, and screens in one place. | [Display](../channels/DISPLAY.md) |
| GA4 | Google Analytics 4 | The current version of Google Analytics, which replaced Universal Analytics. | [Analytics](ANALYTICS.md) |
| RTB | Real-time bidding | Buying a single ad impression through an automatic auction that runs in the moment the page or app loads. | [Display](../channels/DISPLAY.md) |

## Business types

| Term | Stands for | What it means | Where it comes up |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| B2B | Business-to-business | A company that sells to other businesses rather than to consumers. | [Social](../channels/SOCIAL.md) |
| DTC | Direct-to-consumer | A brand that sells straight to customers, usually online, instead of through retailers. | [Social](../channels/SOCIAL.md) |
| SMB | Small and medium-sized business | The businesses these guides are written for. | [Broadcast](../channels/BROADCAST.md) |
