## When editing

- **REPO_OUTLINE.md:** Describe folder layout and catalog generation. Do not maintain a per-file bullet list.
- **COMMUNITY_GUIDELINES.md:** Keep aligned with [../../CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md](../../CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md), which points here. Use the canonical repo URL: `https://github.com/TMHSDigital/Awesome-Ads`.

## Tone

Professional and welcoming. Policy files should be short and unambiguous.

## Do not

- Move community policy to root without updating `CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md` and `CONTRIBUTING.md` links.
- Duplicate full contributing workflows here; link to [../../CONTRIBUTING.md](../../CONTRIBUTING.md) instead.
